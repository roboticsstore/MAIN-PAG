Why Our Open Source Ecosystem is Different

Unlike locked-down commercial robots with expensive monthly subscriptions, our hardware is built entirely open source. You own your hardware for life, completely free of forced vendor fees. Our ecosystem allows you to use a single, universal companion app to manage all your devices seamlessly. Furthermore, you can bring your own AI: easily integrate any Large Language Model, vision model, or custom script directly from Hugging Face, GitHub repositories, or local Ollama/LM Studio instances via standard API endpoints.

An Integrated Ecosystem: From Playtime to Perimeter Defense

Our autonomous systems are incredibly versatile. They are safe and engaging enough for kids to learn STEM concepts and enjoy smart play, yet robust enough to serve as a comprehensive home or business security grid. All of these units are designed to work together as a complete security team[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span).

By deploying both ground and aerial units, you create a layered network. Ground units can patrol indoors and outdoors to detect and confront anomalies. If a suspect attempts to evade ground detection, our aerial drones provide an unblinking eye in the sky. Ultimate Privacy and Local Control

We believe in putting control back in your hands. While standard security companies immediately push data to the cloud or automatically involve law enforcement, our system allows for ultimate privacy. You dictate how your security handles a breach. You might be dealing with an internal employee dispute or a sensitive family matter where you want to catch the culprit without immediately notifying the police[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span). Our ecosystem gives you the power to monitor, gather evidence, and resolve issues privately.

Because the logic is hosted locally, you have total control over advanced features like facial recognition. You can enable facial tracking to recognize approved family members, easily replace the recognition models when needed, or disable the feature entirely to ensure total privacy. Whether you want to actively scare away intruders or quietly track a suspect until you are ready to take action, the Robotic Store puts you in the driver's seat.
